ABOUT / E-NTANGLEMENT

The word entanglement is used in physics to describe the phenomenon in which two or more particles become interconnected, so that the state of one instantly influences the state of another, no matter how far apart they are.

Here, entanglement becomes a metaphor for the dependencies that shape the internet. A space created to connect people has become a place where connections turn into constraints: those who control data, platforms, and infrastructure define the terms of economic and political independence.

The internet is in continuous expansion, and we can only grasp a crystallization of its present condition. This project conceptualizes it as a three-dimensional space that can be explored and sectioned in countless directions. The vertical slice investigated here uses Control as a critical lens and Scale as a narrative line.

E-ntanglement is a critical project that investigates who holds power over the internet and how each of us is entangled within it. The internet is its field of investigation; the interface is the medium through which it is represented. To navigate this topic, I allow myself to get lost in this infinite collection of data, remembering that data never represents objective truth, but that the way it is represented builds only one subjective narrative among many.

Nine different stories share one underlying plot: control has taken over the space created for connection. Who owns the power? What room remains for the people entangled within it? And if the internet is dead, what can still be saved?
