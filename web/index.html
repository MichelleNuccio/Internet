ENTANGLED
DEPENDANCIES

Internet is a planetary phenomena, something so big and complex that can’t be understood in its totality.

There are infinite ways to talk about it but, here, Control is used as a critical lens of investigation for this entangled ecosystem and Scale as a narrative line - from its conception as a global place dominated by agency and protocols where inequalities manifest, to a space inhabited by humans and not-humans actors.

Who owns the power and the informations? And how do we feed everyday the system that controls us?

To investigate this topic, I will allow myself to be lost in this infinite collection of data, remembering that data never represents the objective truth, but that the way in which they are represented build only one subjective narrative among many. In this sense, the final output wouldn’t give a different a total understanding of the internet itself but, will propose [number] different ways to observe the same plot: The internet as battle ground in which dependences between countries, companies and individuals take shape.
